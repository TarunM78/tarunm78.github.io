A planetary actuator for humanoid joints, designed from the start to be metal 3D printed
rather than machined. The exploded view is the whole argument for it: the housing, carrier
and output flange are drawn as printed geometry, so the shapes are chosen for what the
process is good at rather than for what a mill can reach.
