A torso-mounted bimanual arm built for imitation-learning data collection, designed around
the constraint that every joint stays backdrivable and each actuator stays cheap. Six
degrees of freedom per arm plus a servo gripper, roughly 600mm reach, targeting a 1 to 2 kg
payload. Four GIM6010-8 actuators handle the distal joints and two GIM8108-8 carry the
shoulder, with integrated 8:1 planetary reduction rather than a higher gear ratio, since
raising the ratio is the fast way to lose backdrivability. One arm gets built and working
before parts are ordered for the second.
