About a third of stent patients develop restenosis, and a large part of the cause is that
conventional stents expand uniformly into vessels that are not uniform. This design uses an
auxetic lattice, which widens laterally as it stretches, so the stent conforms to irregular
vessel geometry and spreads radial force instead of concentrating it. A bi-stable mechanism
holds both the contracted and expanded states, so the vessel cannot pull the stent back
down. The expanded geometry is fitted per patient from MRI and CT angiography, reconstructed
to CAD, with FEA used to check stress distribution under pulsatile flow.
